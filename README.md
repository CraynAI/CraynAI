# Crayn AI

**Custom AI agents, chatbots, process automation and enterprise software for companies.**

Crayn AI is an Istanbul-based software company that builds custom generative AI systems for businesses. We turn repetitive tasks, scattered data and manual processes into AI-powered software that is measurable and built to scale.

## What we build

- **AI agent development:** agents that take actions inside your CRM, ERP, e-mail and internal tools, not just answer questions.
- **AI chatbots:** customer support and sales assistants trained on your own documents and connected to your systems.
- **Process automation and orchestration:** workflows that move data between teams and tools without manual copy-paste.
- **Custom software:** web applications, mobile apps and B2B platforms built around how your company actually works.

Every project starts with a free discovery call. Projects are delivered in short phases, so you see working software early.
